Emergency dental services in Milwaukee

Every service below is built around one goal: getting you out of pain and back to normal life as quickly as it can safely be done.

Emergency Tooth Extraction

When a tooth is fractured beyond repair, badly decayed, or causing a spreading infection, extraction is sometimes the safest option. We assess the tooth with digital imaging, confirm extraction is necessary, and typically complete the procedure the same day you call, with clear aftercare instructions so healing goes smoothly. Sports injuries, falls, and long-neglected decay are the most common reasons patients need this service, and prompt treatment prevents the problem from spreading to nearby teeth or bone.

Book this service →

Emergency & Same-Day Root Canal

An emergency root canal removes infected or inflamed tissue from inside a tooth, relieving pressure and pain while preserving the natural tooth. It's the treatment of choice when a tooth can still be saved instead of extracted. We prioritize same-day root canal appointments for patients in active pain, using modern numbing techniques to keep the procedure as comfortable as possible from start to finish.

Book this service →

Severe Toothache Relief

Sudden, intense tooth pain is rarely random. It's usually a sign of decay, an abscess, a cracked tooth, or a failed filling or crown. We diagnose the true cause with an exam and digital X-rays, then treat the source of the pain rather than just masking it, so relief actually lasts instead of returning within a day or two.

Book this service →

Urgent Tooth Care

Chipped teeth, lost fillings, popped-off crowns, and cracked enamel all fall under urgent tooth care, and all of them get worse the longer they're left untreated. We stabilize the damage quickly, protect the tooth from further breakdown, and put together a plan for lasting repair, whether that's bonding, a new crown, or another restorative option.

Weekend & Same-Day Appointments

Dental emergencies don't check the calendar before happening, so we don't limit urgent care to weekdays. As a dentist open on Saturday, and available Sunday too, we make sure a weekend injury or a Friday-night toothache never turns into three days of unnecessary pain waiting for Monday.

24hr Dental Emergency Line

Our phones are answered around the clock by people trained to triage real dental emergencies, not a generic answering service. Call any hour of the day or night, describe what's happening, and we'll walk you through immediate steps while getting you on the schedule as fast as possible.

Tooth Abscess & Infection Care

A swollen jaw, throbbing pain, or a bad taste near a tooth can signal a dental abscess, and it can spread quickly if left untreated. We diagnose the infection with an exam and digital imaging, drain or treat it as needed, and prescribe medication when appropriate so the infection is brought under control fast.

Book this service →

Lost Crown & Filling Repair

A crown or filling that comes loose leaves the tooth exposed and vulnerable to further damage and pain. We re-cement, repair, or replace the restoration the same day whenever possible, protecting the tooth underneath and getting you back to eating and drinking comfortably.

Book this service →

Knocked-Out & Dislodged Tooth Care

A tooth that's been fully or partially knocked out of its socket needs attention within the first hour for the best chance of saving it. Call us immediately, keep the tooth moist, and come straight in. Our team will assess whether the tooth can be re-implanted or splinted, and stabilize the area to prevent further damage.
